Who Were the Signers?

Famous Signers

Some of the most famous signers of the Declaration of Independence include

John Hancock
President of the Continental Congress. Famous for his enormous, distinctive signature, which made his name synonymous with signing your name.
Thomas Jefferson
Principal author of the Declaration of Independence. Later became the third U.S. president.
Benjamin Franklin
Inventor, scientist, diplomat, and one of the most influential figures in the American Revolution.
John Adams
Important advocate for independence and later the second U.S. president.
Samuel Adams
Revolutionary organizer and a major figure in the resistance to British rule.
Roger Sherman
The only person to sign all four major founding documents: the Continental Association, Declaration of Independence, Articles of Confederation, and U.S. Constitution.
Robert Morris
Financier who helped fund the American Revolution and became known as the "Financier of the Revolution."
John Witherspoon
Minister, educator, and president of what is now Princeton University.
