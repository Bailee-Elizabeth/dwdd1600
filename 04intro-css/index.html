Health Benefits of Tea

Tea is rich in antioxidants, which can help protect your cells from damage. It may also support heart health, improve mental alertness, and aid in digestion. Drinking tea regularly can contribute to overall well-being.

Useful vocabulary terms

Here are some important terms related to tea:

Steeping
The process of brewing tea by adding hot water to tea leaves.
Infusion
The process of extracting flavors from tea leaves by soaking them in hot water.
Oxidation
The chemical process that occurs when tea leaves are exposed to air, affecting their flavor and color.

Tea shouldn't taste like grass!

Some teas are more sensitive to brewing conditions than others.

  • Green teas: are delicate and can become bitter if steeped for too long or at too high a temperature.
  • Black teas: are more robust and can handle longer steeping times and higher temperatures.
  • Herbal teas: vary widely in flavor and may require different brewing methods depending on the ingredients used.

Water Temperature Guide

Water temperature changes how a tea tastes. Here are the types listed from coolest water to hottest:

  1. Green tea: 160-180°F
  2. White tea: 170-185°F
  3. Oolong tea: 185-205°F
  4. Black tea: 200-212°F
  5. Herbal tea: 208-212°F
